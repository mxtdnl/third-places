[TC] p. 7 · Interventions

Why Design Opens Only Now

“we deliberately ask groups to hold off on interventions”

Taplin & Clark · [TC] p. 7

Weeks 9 and 10 settled what needs to happen: the long-term outcome and the preconditions beneath it. Only now does the group ask what the club would do.

Holding off keeps favourite ideas out until they have earned a place. “This way, only those interventions that best contribute to the accomplishment of outcomes are included in the theory.” ([TC] p. 7)

Risk or Assumption?

Assumption
In the register · [TC] p. 6
“assumptions are already in place and do not need to be brought about”. It needs evidence that it holds in Bodø today.
Risk
In the risk register · [GAF] p. 27
“Risks are internal or external factors that can influence your programme or project either positively or negatively.” It may or may not happen: give it a likelihood and a mitigation.

If an assumption could stop being true, that is a risk
