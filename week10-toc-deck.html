Two Readings, One Word

What Counts as an Assumption

“assumptions are already in place and do not need to be brought about”

Taplin & Clark · [TC] p. 6

[GAF] p. 23 uses “assumption” more broadly. Its assumptions can relate to “the links between inputs, outputs, and outcomes”: beliefs about the arrows themselves.

This course follows Taplin and Clark, as Activity 7 does. The “why” on each arrow is a rationale. An assumption is a condition already in place. Today we keep them apart, because “wrong assumptions can undermine your theory of change.” ([TC] p. 6)

Arrow: rationale · Register: assumption
