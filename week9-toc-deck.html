Taplin & Clark · [TC] p. 3

Every later week hangs from this sentence. Week 10 maps backwards from it. Week 11 places interventions that serve it. Week 12 writes the indicators that will show whether it holds. Week 13 puts it into the recommendation's “so that”.

Before You Write the Outcome

Whose Community?

Oldenburg’s third place is a setting for informal sociability among individuals. People come by choice, on neutral ground where nobody plays host. Inside, the place is a leveller: rank and status from outside count for little.

A club can mean something else by “community”. Your outcome has to say which community it serves.

The Question Every Group Answers

When the client says “community”, whom does it mean? Is that the community of a third place?

Match fans, residents of one area, the whole town, people who never come to a match: each answer gives a different long-term outcome, and different stakeholders.

Read the client brief (on the VLE). Write its meaning in your own words, with the slide number. The workbench does not store the brief.
